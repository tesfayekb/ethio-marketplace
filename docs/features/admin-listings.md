# Admin › Listings (the Listings console)

Bundle 11, the Listings console (D134, D136, D137; spec: docs/governance/listings-console-spec.md). Turn L1 built the doors and the seller's notices; turn L2 the All listings page and the Listings group in the side menu; the row's actions, the tick-boxes and the Edit form come in the next turns.

## The doors (turn L1)

- **`admin_list_listings(view, search, country, category, seller, from, to, tier, limit, offset)`** — STABLE definer, `listings:view`. Drafts never. Views: `live` (active, reduced), `waiting` (screening, held), `ended` (expired, sold), `removed` (rejected, removed), `all`. Search: a title's words, or a listing's id. Filters: the country of the listing's place, a category and everything under it, the seller (public name, a business's name; the e-mail too for `profiles:view` holders), the date posted (from, to), the tier. Newest first; at most 100 a page; each row carries the total. A row: the listing, its status and tier, whether it carries the severe screening flag, the seller's public name (and business name), the e-mail only for `profiles:view` holders, the category and place names in both languages, the price fields, the cover photo's paths, the photo count and the dates. Refusals: "not signed in", "permission denied", "unknown view", "unknown tier".
- **`admin_edit_listing(listing, changes, reason, note)`** — VOLATILE definer, `listings:enforce` with a fresh second factor. `changes` holds any of title, description, category_id, attributes, the price fields (price_mode, price_amount, price_currency, price_period, price_bp, price_negotiable) and `remove_photos` (photo ids); anything else is refused ("not editable: <key>"). It refuses a draft ("listing not found"), a removed listing and the admin's own listing. The seller's content rules decide (the validator through step 7): a refusal answers `{ok: false, refusals}`, the seller's own shape, and writes nothing. A live listing stays live. One edit writes one `listing_revisions` row (kind `edit`, before and after, the admin as actor; a removed photo's whole row is kept in `before.photos_removed`), one `audit_log` row (`listing.admin_edit`: the fields, the reason, the note) and one seller notice. The same values again answer "nothing changed" and write nothing.
- **`admin_move_listing(listing, to, reason, note)`** — VOLATILE definer, `listings:enforce` with a fresh second factor. Remove (any status but draft and removed → removed), Restore (removed → screening; a listing with the severe screening flag by a super admin only) and Send back to review (active or reduced → screening). Any other move: "illegal move: <from> -> <to>". Through `transition_listing`, which writes the state revision; then one `audit_log` row (`listing.admin_remove`, `listing.admin_restore` or `listing.admin_send_back`: from, to, reason, note) and one seller notice.
- **`transition_listing`** gains Restore: removed → screening, enforcement only, with a fresh second factor; a severe removal by a super admin only (REQ-022 as amended by D137). The seller cannot restore a removed listing.

## The page (turn L2)

- **The side menu:** a **Listings** group (its own glyph), first in the menu, holding **All listings** (`/admin/listings`, `listings:view`) and the **Screening queue** (moved under it; its address stays `/admin/screening`, its permission `listings:review`). Like Catalog, the group has no page of its own; each item keeps its own gate, so a moderator (who holds `listings:view` and not `listings:review`) sees All listings and not the queue.
- **All listings** (`src/features/admin-listings/listings-page.tsx`, route `src/routes/admin.listings.tsx`) on the agreed blocks: the heading in the header with the page's one line; the views as pills above the toolbar — All (the default), Live, Waiting, Ended, Removed; the toolbar — search (a title's words, or a listing's id), Filters (the country of the listing's place, a top category with everything under it, the seller, posted from / until with the house date field, the tier), Columns (the title locked); the chips under it; the footer (range, rows per page kept in this browser, pages). The server pages the list (`useAdminListings`, one call of `admin_list_listings` a page; the previous page stays on screen while the next loads). Newest first.
- **The row:** title, seller (the public name — a business's name when the seller posts as a business — and the e-mail under it for `profiles:view` holders, cut short with the whole address on hover), place · country, category, price, the status pill, posted, ends. Cards up to 1024 px (a dense roster, as on Screening); from 1024 px the table, with the category and the end date from 1280 px. In the table a date shows day and month (the year only when it is not this year), the full date on hover; the card shows day, month and year.
- **Not yet:** the row's Open, Edit and three-dots (Send back, Remove, Restore, View as seller) and the tick-boxes come in the next turns; this page reads only. The spec's photo on the row waits for photo serving: the door returns the cover's storage references, and no page renders a stored photo yet (the feed's card passes no photo either).

## The row's moves and the tick-boxes (turn L2b)

- **The row's three-dots** (`listing-actions-<id>`): Send back to review (a live listing: active or reduced), Restore (a removed listing, in any view), View as seller (holders of `impersonation:use`: the existing impersonation form, with its reason and second factor) and, last and red, Remove (any listing that is not removed). Moves need `listings:enforce`; the admin's own listings get no move (the door changes them the seller's way). A holder of `listings:view` without `listings:enforce` or `impersonation:use` sees no three-dots and no tick-boxes.
- **The move dialog** (`listings-move-dialog`, src/features/admin-listings/move-dialog.tsx): what the move does; the reason, from the door's own list for that move (src/features/admin-listings/move-reasons.ts, checked against the L1 migration by MR-1); a note for the seller, required for Other, at most 500 characters. Nothing is sent until both are as the door wants them; the door's refusal is shown in the dialog and nothing closes on it (F4).
- **Tick-boxes** (D136 4): the selection bar offers Remove for the ticked listings that can be removed and Restore for the ticked removed ones; one dialog and one second factor for the set, then the door once per listing, each with its own audit row and notice. A refusal of one listing does not stop the others (the rate dial's refusal stops the set); the line above the table counts what moved and names the first refusal. The ticks clear when the view, the search, a filter or the page changes.
- **The layout:** with the tick-boxes and the three-dots the table needs a wide desktop, so cards run up to 1280 px and the table starts there (`cardUntil="xl"`; L2 had 1024 px).
- **Strings changed by the operator (2026-10-11):** "Live" (the view and the status pill) «በቀጥታ»; "Posted" «የተለጠፈ».

## One listing's page (turn L3)

The operator's choice (D139, 2026-10-11): a page per listing with the full history of its changes; Edit follows in turn L4.

- **The door:** `admin_listing_detail(listing)` — STABLE definer, `listings:view`; a draft answers "listing not found". One answer: the listing (content, status, tier, the severe flag, place and country, category, price, photo count, and its dates: created, first posted, last posted, ends, the seller's own end date, renewals, last changed); the seller (public name, a business's name, member since, the account's state; the e-mail only for `profiles:view`); the contact methods the seller shows, as true or false; and the history.
- **The history:** the listing's revisions, newest first (at most 200, with the total): when, the kind, who acted (the seller, a staff member, or the system — a staff member's name only for `audit_logs:view`), the columns changed, the values before and after for the content, price, status, place, tier and dates only — an exact pin, an address and the contact settings are named, never shown — and a removed photo counted, never its storage path; the reason and note an admin gave, from the seller's notice written in the same transaction.
- **The page** (`/admin/listings/<id>`, src/features/admin-listings/listing-page.tsx; opened by the title in All listings or Open, first in every row's three-dots): back to All listings; the heading with the status pill (the tier and the severe flag when they apply); Preview as buyer (the buyer's view, as Screening shows it); the moves the person holds (Send back, Restore, Remove — the same dialog as the list) and View as seller; Details; History — one line per revision ("Status: Live → Removed", "Edited: Title, Price", "Created", "Renewed"), who, the reason and the note, and for an edit "Show changes" with each value before and after. Details and History sit side by side from 1024 px.
- A holder of `listings:view` alone sees the page without the e-mail, the account link, the moves and View as seller; the row's three-dots then holds Open alone.

## Edit: the title, the description and the price (turn L4a)

The operator's choice (D140, 2026-10-11): Edit in two turns — L4a the title, the description and the price; L4b the category and the specifications. Removing a photo waits until the console shows photos.

- **Edit** (`listing-edit`) on the listing's page, for `listings:enforce`, never on the admin's own listing or a removed one (the door's rules). It opens the form in place of Details and History (src/features/admin-listings/edit-form.tsx): the title (at most 120 characters), the description (at most 5,000), the price — the seller's own price form (`StepPricing`) with the category's price rules (`get_posting_schema`) and the basis the listing's specifications put in force; a price with none opens on the listing's market money — then the reason (Misleading details, Contact details in the text, The site's rules, Other) and a note for the seller (required for Other, at most 500 characters; the same block as the moves, `ReasonNote`), Cancel and Save changes.
- **What travels:** only what changed (`editChanges`, src/features/admin-listings/edit-listing.ts); a price travels whole, as the door stores it (an amount and a currency only for a fixed price, a percentage only for a commission). Nothing changed is said on the form and calls no door.
- **The answer:** the door judges the edit with the seller's content rules for what an admin can change (DEC-173); a refusal lands under its field, and one naming a field the form does not draw (the category, a specification) is listed above Save. Saved: the form closes, the line above says what the seller is told ("Saved. The seller is told what changed: Price, Title."), and History's newest line is the edit with its reason and note.
- **DEC-173 / INC-552 (the migration):** `admin_edit_listing` judged through the contact step (7) with the listing's end date and places, so a listing whose seller set an end date within a day (or one already past), whose place was later switched off, or whose contact settings fail today's rules could not be corrected at all. It now judges through step 5 with no end date and no places.
- **INC-553 — a price with its unit:** every price on the console prints with its unit ("1,450,000 ETB per bag"), as the card does: All listings (the door `admin_list_listings` now carries `price_unit` and `price_unit_text`), the listing's page (Details, History's values, Preview as buyer) and the Screening queue's preview.

## Edit: the category and the specifications (turn L4b)

- **The category** (`listing-edit-category`): the path, and "Change category", which opens the seller's own tree with its filter (`StepCategory`; the admin's own recent categories are not offered — its new `showRecent` option); choosing a leaf closes it and the form follows the new category: its specifications, its price rules. A moved listing keeps the answers its new category also asks; the door releases the others (`validate_listing_attributes`), and the form says so.
- **The specifications** (`listing-edit-specs`): the seller's own form (`StepSpecifications`) for the category in the form; the "How it is sold" rows are drawn with the price, as on the seller's price page. A refusal lands under its own control (the door's `attr_key` is the control's key); one naming a question the form does not draw is listed above Save as "Specifications".
- **What travels:** the category when another leaf was chosen; the specifications when they differ from the listing's AND the admin changed the category or worked in them — the seller's form fills a question's default and tidies hidden answers on its own, and an admin who only looked sends none of that.
- **The reasons** gain Wrong category.
- **INC-554 — the breadcrumb:** on every admin page below a section's own (a user, a role, a listing, a language's strings), the section is a link back to it; the listing's page ends with the listing's title and a language's page with the language's own name. Before, only the user and role pages linked back. The two new crumbs load only on their pages (src/components/shell/admin-detail-crumbs.tsx, a lazy module), so the first paint grows by about 1 KiB.

## The seller's notices

`seller_notices`: the seller, the listing, the kind (`admin_edit`, `admin_remove`, `admin_restore`, `admin_send_back`), the fields as the seller reads them (title, description, category, specs, price, photos; status for a move), the reason, the note, when, and when read. Row security: the seller reads only their own; no browser role writes. Nothing shows them yet: My ads (stage 4, D138) and notifications (stage 6) will.
