# The ad page (D201)

The page `/l/<words>-<public_id>` is built in bundle 19 (the spec: Project record D201 v1, 2026-10-17; the study of 2026-10-16). This turn lands the address rules and the QR route only.

## The address

The public id is ten characters of `23456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstuvwxyz`, generated by the database at insert; this lands with M1 in turn 4. The canonical address is `/l/<words>-<id>`, with the words from the title's ASCII fold (≤ 60 characters) and a 301 to the canonical when the words differ. `/l/<id>` alone also resolves and is what the QR carries; these page resolutions arrive in a later turn.

`/l/<id>/qr.svg` lands this turn: an immutable SVG from `qrcode-generator` 2.0.4, server-only, validated by id shape, with no database read. It carries the request origin's short address `/l/<id>`.

## The address helper (turn 3)

`src/features/ad-page/address.ts` is the one place the address is built and read, pure and client-safe (no import, no database, no browser API): `isPublicId` and the alphabet (moved from `src/server/qr.ts`, which re-exports it); `addressWords(title)` — NFKD with the combining marks removed, lower case, runs of characters other than a–z 0–9 become one `-`, no `-` at either end, at most 60 characters cut on a `-` boundary; `canonicalSegment` / `listingPath` → `/l/<words>-<id>`, or `/l/<id>` when the words are empty (an Amharic title); `shortListingPath` → `/l/<id>`; `listingUrl(origin, …)`; `parseAddressSegment(segment)` → the id at the end of the segment and the words before it, or `null`. The route (turn 5) parses, reads by the id, and answers a 301 to the canonical when the words differ.

## The read and the counters (M1, turn 4)

The migration of turn 4 (its mark `20261018130000`) gives every ad a `public_id` — ten characters of the alphabet above, drawn by `new_public_id()` at insert, backfilled for every existing row, unique, shape-checked — and three kinds of door:

- `public_listing(p_public_id)` — the ad page's ONE read, callable by anon and authenticated. It restates the public RLS predicate (`listings_public_read`: live, or sold within 30 days) as its own WHERE and answers `null` for anything else (a draft, removed, expired, long-sold or unknown id all look the same). A signed-in caller passes `rate_gate('listing_read')` (600 an hour); an anonymous caller is counted by the route, by hashed address. The document (built by `public_listing_json`, service-role only, proven on an in-memory row): `public_id`, `title`, `description`, `status`, `is_sold`, `sold_at`, `sold_word` (sold / rented / filled by the category's capabilities), `reserved`, `published_at`, `expires_at`, `renewed_at`, `tier`, `photos_soon`, `video_url`, `country` (the primary place's); `price` {mode, amount, currency, period, bp, negotiable, unit, unit_text, unit_label {en, am}, was and drop_at (only within 14 days of the drop, as the card)}; `category` {id, slug, name_en, name_am, capabilities, illustration_url (the nearest ancestor's picture), deal (the price page's groups), path (root → leaf: id, slug, name_en, name_am)}; `attributes` (the answers as stored); `definitions` (only the ANSWERED facts, each as `get_posting_schema` serves a row, plus `options` — the records of the options the answer names, with their labels in both languages); `photos` (stripped and not held, in display order: id, display_order, paths, width, height) and `cover_photo_id`; `places` {location_id, coverage (ids), list (id, name_en, name_am, level, slug, country_code, position, map_visible), pin {lat, lng, precision, zoom} or null when the ad has no shown coordinates — the SHOWN coordinates only (`pin_show_lat/lng`, rounded for an approximate pin by `set_listing_pin`; zoom null when approximate), directions, street_address}; `seller` {alias, business_name (business sellers only), previous_alias (within 365 days), member_since}; `contact_channels` (the names of the channels the seller shows — phone, phone2, telegram, whatsapp — never a value); `counts` {views, shares}; `viewer` {owner}. It never carries a phone, chat handle or e-mail, the seller's user id, `home_country_code`, `screening`, or `pin_lat` / `pin_lng`.
- `count_listing_view(p_public_id, p_rate_key, p_viewer)` and `count_listing_share(p_public_id, p_channel, p_rate_key, p_viewer)` — service-role only, called by server routes that hash the caller's address into `p_rate_key`: one count per address and ad (and channel) per hour through `consume_rate_limit`; the owner's own view is not counted; the channel is one of telegram, whatsapp, facebook, copy, qr; the buckets are `listing_counters` (one row per ad and day; RLS on, no client privilege — the deny policy `listing_counters_no_client`).
- `listing_counts(p_listing_id)` — the owner's sums on `auth.uid()` (views, shares, shares_by_channel, contacts from `contact_reveals`); "not your listing" for anyone else.

The backfill paused the two triggers that fire on any update (`update_listings_updated_at`, `feed_index_on_listing_update`) for its one statement, so no ad's `updated_at` moved and no live ad was re-indexed; the proofs check both are enabled again. The migration travels the file route (DEC-182: longer than 12 KB): the operator applies it from the raw file on ethio-staging, then ethio-prod, each read back by the mark.

## The page (turn 5)

`/l/$slug` now reads the public document on the server through the GET server function `readAdPageFn`, validating a non-empty slug; the server answers gone before parsing a slug longer than 200 characters. `readAdPage` parses the address before work, then spends the shared address dial `listing-read:address`: 600 calls an hour, overridden by `RATE_LIMIT_LISTING_READ_PER_HOUR`. It uses `clientAddress` and `consumeRate` (the database hashes the address key). The publishable-key `anonymousClient` is moved unchanged from photo-links into user-client.ts; no session or privileged listing read is used.

The loader returns the ad at its canonical address, sends a 301 when the short address or old words differ, and throws not-found for missing, private or long-sold ads. A spent dial logs `[ssr-error] /l rateLimited` and also gives the gone page (404). Failed public reads log and throw, never pretending the ad is gone. The gone page has its own title, caption and Home link, from the three supplied `adPage.gone.*` keys in English and Amharic.

The head carries the ad's title followed by ethio.com, the first 160 characters of its description, canonical and Open Graph addresses on the request origin, and `noindex` for a sold ad. `adPageView` is the one pure mapping from the public document onto the shared `ListingDetail`, inside the seller page's same PageShell and PageCard: embedded price basis, deal groups, answered definitions and option labels, public photos, seller line, shown pin and contact channel names only. The existing option shaper is exported as `shapeAttrOption`, and `shapeDeal` is exported unchanged. The ribbon key is translated by the page.

The `/l/` breadcrumb reads only the title and leaf id from the route's loaded document and lazily loads its own crumb module: Home › the category path from the shared tree › the ad title. Until the tree loads it shows Home › title; for the gone page, Home alone. It adds no second title truncation rule.

Not yet on this page: the counters line, sharing, the contact reveal action, similar ads and the key-facts strip. The feed card and the seller's link await turn 6's public-id reads; the address is the entry this turn.

Turn 6 — INC-678 fixes option lists to use `attr_key` (each reader's key), including an empty list for a number definition, and maps a null shown pin to no pin. An address longer than 200 characters answers the gone page, not an error. M1b (`feed_page`, `my_listings`, `my_listing`, mark `20261018140000`) carries `publicId`; older answers parse it as null. The feed card's content is one keyboard-focusable `listing-card-link` to `/l/<words>-<id>` with no nested controls; without the id its layout stays unchanged. The seller's actions offer `my-listing-open-public`, opening the buyer's view in a new tab for active or sold ads only. DEC-188 overrides the file route for this migration: applied on ethio-prod by the migration tool, with P1–P3 passing and the attachment's SHA-256 preserved; the operator applies the saved file on ethio-staging. AP-4 now asserts the stronger anonymous permission refusal. PW-162(d) waits for the failed-send notice to clear after the hook's successful retry before Back, without changing its observations, timeout or retry policy.

Turn 7 — `ListingDetail` carries `actor` (buyer, owner, staff) and every mount names its own; the owner (the seller's ad page, the wizard's Review) sees his own channel values as text and, on his ad page, "N views" from `listing_counts` — only what is counted is drawn (views now; shares with the share sheet; contacts with the contact block; Saves at M3), the unavailable line when the read fails, nothing for an ad that was never live; the buyer and staff never see a value or a count; the Messages row is gone until messaging exists (ruling 8); a view is counted by `POST /api/listings/view` from the page after it renders — the route's own dial `listing-view:address` (600 an hour, `RATE_LIMIT_LISTING_VIEW_PER_HOUR`), then `count_listing_view` with the hashed address and the viewer's id when the bearer names one, so the owner's visits and a second visit from one address within the hour do not count, and a crawler without JavaScript does not count; the public page does not show its counts yet (turn 13).
