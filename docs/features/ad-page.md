# The ad page (D201)

The page `/l/<words>-<public_id>` is built in bundle 19 (the spec: Project record D201 v1, 2026-10-17; the study of 2026-10-16). This turn lands the address rules and the QR route only.

## The address

The public id is ten characters of `23456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstuvwxyz`, generated by the database at insert; this lands with M1 in turn 4. The canonical address is `/l/<words>-<id>`, with the words from the title's ASCII fold (≤ 60 characters) and a 301 to the canonical when the words differ. `/l/<id>` alone also resolves and is what the QR carries; these page resolutions arrive in a later turn.

`/l/<id>/qr.svg` lands this turn: an immutable SVG from `qrcode-generator` 2.0.4, server-only, validated by id shape, with no database read. It carries the request origin's short address `/l/<id>`.

## The address helper (turn 3)

`src/features/ad-page/address.ts` is the one place the address is built and read, pure and client-safe (no import, no database, no browser API): `isPublicId` and the alphabet (moved from `src/server/qr.ts`, which re-exports it); `addressWords(title)` — NFKD with the combining marks removed, lower case, runs of characters other than a–z 0–9 become one `-`, no `-` at either end, at most 60 characters cut on a `-` boundary; `canonicalSegment` / `listingPath` → `/l/<words>-<id>`, or `/l/<id>` when the words are empty (an Amharic title); `shortListingPath` → `/l/<id>`; `listingUrl(origin, …)`; `parseAddressSegment(segment)` → the id at the end of the segment and the words before it, or `null`. The route (turn 5) parses, reads by the id, and answers a 301 to the canonical when the words differ.

## The read and the counters (M1, turn 4)

The migration of turn 4 (its mark `20261018130000`) gives every ad a `public_id` — ten characters of the alphabet above, drawn by `new_public_id()` at insert, backfilled for every existing row, unique, shape-checked — and three kinds of door:

- `public_listing(p_public_id)` — the ad page's ONE read, callable by anon and authenticated. It restates the public RLS predicate (`listings_public_read`: live, or sold within 30 days) as its own WHERE and answers `null` for anything else (a draft, removed, expired, long-sold or unknown id all look the same). A signed-in caller passes `rate_gate('listing_read')` (600 an hour); an anonymous caller is counted by the route, by hashed address. The document (built by `public_listing_json`, service-role only, proven on an in-memory row): `public_id`, `title`, `description`, `status`, `is_sold`, `sold_at`, `sold_word` (sold / rented / filled by the category's capabilities), `reserved`, `published_at`, `expires_at`, `renewed_at`, `tier`, `photos_soon`, `video_url`, `country` (the primary place's); `price` {mode, amount, currency, period, bp, negotiable, unit, unit_text, unit_label {en, am}, was and drop_at (only within 14 days of the drop, as the card)}; `category` {id, slug, name_en, name_am, capabilities, illustration_url (the nearest ancestor's picture), deal (the price page's groups), path (root → leaf: id, slug, name_en, name_am)}; `attributes` (the answers as stored); `definitions` (only the ANSWERED facts, each as `get_posting_schema` serves a row, plus `options` — the records of the options the answer names, with their labels in both languages); `photos` (stripped and not held, in display order: id, display_order, paths, width, height) and `cover_photo_id`; `places` {location_id, coverage (ids), list (id, name_en, name_am, level, slug, country_code, position, map_visible), pin {lat, lng, precision, zoom} — the SHOWN coordinates only (`pin_show_lat/lng`, rounded for an approximate pin by `set_listing_pin`; zoom null when approximate), directions, street_address}; `seller` {alias, business_name (business sellers only), previous_alias (within 365 days), member_since}; `contact_channels` (the names of the channels the seller shows — phone, phone2, telegram, whatsapp — never a value); `counts` {views, shares}; `viewer` {owner}. It never carries a phone, chat handle or e-mail, the seller's user id, `home_country_code`, `screening`, or `pin_lat` / `pin_lng`.
- `count_listing_view(p_public_id, p_rate_key, p_viewer)` and `count_listing_share(p_public_id, p_channel, p_rate_key, p_viewer)` — service-role only, called by server routes that hash the caller's address into `p_rate_key`: one count per address and ad (and channel) per hour through `consume_rate_limit`; the owner's own view is not counted; the channel is one of telegram, whatsapp, facebook, copy, qr; the buckets are `listing_counters` (one row per ad and day; RLS on, no client privilege — the deny policy `listing_counters_no_client`).
- `listing_counts(p_listing_id)` — the owner's sums on `auth.uid()` (views, shares, shares_by_channel, contacts from `contact_reveals`); "not your listing" for anyone else.

The backfill paused the two triggers that fire on any update (`update_listings_updated_at`, `feed_index_on_listing_update`) for its one statement, so no ad's `updated_at` moved and no live ad was re-indexed; the proofs check both are enabled again. The migration travels the file route (DEC-182: longer than 12 KB): the operator applies it from the raw file on ethio-staging, then ethio-prod, each read back by the mark.
