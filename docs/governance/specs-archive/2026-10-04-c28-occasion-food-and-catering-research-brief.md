C28 — OCCASION FOOD AND CATERING: RESEARCH AND PLAN (no import rows in this delivery)

WHY
The operator sees many businesses that prepare food for occasions. They sell by the number of items and the number of guests. His notes, to be answered by evidence: fasting, non-fasting or a mixture; the kind of food (alicha wot, key wot, others); for how many plates; whether they bring it; how early it must be ordered; how the price is built.

WHAT THE CATALOGUE HAS TODAY (batch 19 exports; say so if you no longer hold them)
- services › events-services "Events & Catering": service_type-events-services (catering, coffee_ceremony, tent_chair_rental, decoration, dj_music, sound_lighting, mc_host, wedding_planning, other); pricing_type limited to fixed | quote; capacity_guests (10–5000, shown for catering and tent rental); provider_type; availability; certified (catering only); insured.
- food-drink › injera-bakery (unit_of_sale-food per_piece | per_pack | per_dozen | other; fasting_friendly) and other-food.
- travel › restaurants-cafes (pricing_type-travel per_person | quote; cuisine; fasting_menu; delivery_available) and travel › event-venues.
A caterer today can say only: catering, fixed or quote, up to N guests.

WHAT THE FORM CAN CARRY (so the plan is buildable)
- Row types: single_select, multi_select, number (unit, min, max), boolean, text.
- A row can be shown only when another answer has given values; from the next migration also when two answers hold together.
- An option can prefill or narrow other rows.
- One price per ad. The price basis is a pricing_type… or unit_of_sale… list, and the ad reads "<amount> <currency> per <unit>" (per person, per plate, per kg, per tray).
- Rows whose key starts with term_ are asked on the price page and shown under the price as "label: value". Minimum guests, order-ahead days and deposit fit here.
- The title is built from the card-ranked answers.
- Not possible today: several packages with different prices in one ad, a calendar of free dates, ordering or payment. If the evidence says one of these is essential, say so with counts; it becomes a form request, not a row.

PART 1 — RESEARCH
Read real posts by real sellers. At least 120 posts, of which at least 50 from Telegram channels and groups and at least 30 from diaspora sellers. Cover Ethiopia (Addis Ababa and regional cities) and the diaspora cities of the open markets, Eritrean sellers included.
Sources: Telegram (public previews), Facebook pages, groups and Marketplace, Instagram, TikTok, Jiji and other local classifieds, and business listings for Ethiopian and Eritrean catering abroad. For structure only, also read how catering is listed on the large service marketplaces.
For each source give the link, the date read and the number of posts counted. If a source could not be read (login wall, private group), say so. No estimate stands in for a count.
Copy no phone numbers, names or handles. Quote wording only as short anonymised snippets.
Search seeds, not findings: ድግስ, የድግስ ምግብ, ኬተሪንግ, ሰርግ, መልስ, ክርስትና, ልደት, ምርቃት, ተዝካር, ሰደቃ, ዶሮ ወጥ, ቀይ ወጥ, አልጫ ወጥ, ክትፎ, በያይነቱ, ድፎ ዳቦ, "Ethiopian catering" with city names, and the Tigrinya terms you know.

Tally, per post:
1. Who sells: home cook, caterer, restaurant or hotel doing outside catering, bakery, butcher, injera supplier, cook for hire, equipment rental.
2. What is sold: full service at the event; food made and delivered; single dishes to order; counted items (injera, bread, cakes, sambusa); a coffee ceremony; staff only; pots, plates and serving equipment.
3. Occasion named.
4. Fasting, non-fasting or both. Any religious statement about the meat or the kitchen.
5. Dishes named.
6. How quantity is stated: per guest, per plate, per pot, per tray, per kg, per piece, a package for N guests, other.
7. Price: stated or "call". The amounts and currency when stated. One price or a list of packages.
8. Minimum and maximum guests or order.
9. How early to order.
10. What is included: delivery, set-up, serving staff, plates and cutlery, tent, drinks.
11. Area served.
12. Deposit and payment terms.
13. Licence or health-certificate claims.
14. Alcohol offered (tej, tella, areke, beer, wine). Count it; it is not allowed on the site.

1. Placement. Where these ads go: stay inside Events & Catering, a leaf of their own, a Food & Beverages leaf for made-to-order occasion food, or a split. Give the rule a seller uses to pick, the names in English and Amharic, and what is surfaced where.

3. The price: which basis each kind of seller needs, and which term_ rows (with unit and bounds).

STANDING RULES
- Only card rows are required, three at most; every other row is optional.
- A fact settled by an earlier answer is not asked.
- Religious, dietary and licence rows are worded as the seller's own statement; an unticked box claims nothing.
- No wording tied to one country.
- No alcohol options.
- Effort follows real trade volume: a row or a leaf needs posts behind it.
