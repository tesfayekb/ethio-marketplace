## Home country: editable, and residency is not affected

- **The two facts are separate.** The country a seller declares only sets their defaults, such as currency. The country recorded by the server at their first post is what each ad is filed under, and the seller cannot edit that one. So letting sellers change the declared country does not touch residency.
- **The rule I am adding:** the first choice is free, and after that the seller can change it once every 30 days. Each change is written to the audit log, and admins still see both countries.
- **Seeing the full list:** once this lands, your locked box gets a "Change" control, so you can open the list and check it.
