# Mechanisms — the register of concerns (G0.9)

Seeded at bundle 22's records turn (turn 31, 2026-10-23) from the systems audit of 2026-10-23 (the Project doc claude/systems-audit-2026-10-23.md; seven read-only studies at `a413f952`) and the landings of turns 29 and 30. One row per CONCERN — the thing that must behave the same everywhere — naming its one MECHANISM (file), its CONFORMANCE GUARD (the census or lint that fails when a consumer bypasses it), its recorded EXCEPTIONS and the growth axes it already takes (languages, countries, currencies, roles, channels, AI features, deployment targets). Every brief's THE SYSTEM header (instructions v1.14, G0) cites a row here or adds one; a mechanism not in this register does not exist for a brief. A row marked NONE YET names the concern whose mechanism the Foundations bundle builds (DEC-237); its "today" column names what carries the concern until then. Rows are added through the records turn of the bundle that lands the mechanism (G41); nothing here is edited by hand outside one.
