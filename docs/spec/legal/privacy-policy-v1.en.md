# Privacy Policy

Draft version 1. Not yet in force. The English text is the authoritative one.

## 1. Who we are

ethio.com is operated by Ethio.com LLC, a limited liability company organized in the State of Georgia, United States, with its address at 300 Colonial Center Parkway, Ste 100N, Roswell, GA 30076, USA. We decide how and why personal data is used on the service. For any question about your data, write to privacy@ethio.com.

## 2. What we collect

| Kind | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Your email address, or a reference to your Google or Telegram account. A phone number if Telegram gives us one it has verified. Your sign-in methods. |
| Profile | First and last name, seller name, whether you sell as a person or a business and the business name, home country, language, and the contact channels you choose to show (phone, WhatsApp, Telegram). |
| Ads | Everything in an ad: text, the answers you give about the item, price, photos, a video link, the places where it is shown, and a map pin and street line if you add them. |
| Messages | The in-app messages you send and receive. |
| Approximate location | A country and area guessed from your network address on each visit, so that the site opens on ads near you. It is shown on the screen, you can change it, and the guess is not stored. |
| Usage | Counts of views by category and area. |
| Technical records | Your network address (IP), your browser and device type, and the time. These are recorded for each sign-in session and in the server logs of the companies that host the service. To limit abuse we also keep a one-way code made from your network address, not the address itself. |
| Agreements and decisions | The versions of the Terms and of this policy that you accepted, and when. The statements you certified for each ad. Screening and enforcement decisions about your ads and your account. |

## 3. What we do not collect

- **Date of birth or identity documents.** We ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older, and nothing more.
- **Your position, unless you ask.** We read your device's position only when you tap "Use my location" while placing a map pin. It moves the pin, and it is stored only if you save the pin, as part of the ad.
- **Hidden data in photos.** Location and device details embedded in a photo are removed when you upload it.
- **Phone checks by SMS.** We do not verify phone numbers by text message.
- **Payment details.** We take no payments.

## 4. How we use it

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis where one is required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run your account and show your ads | Account, profile, ads | Our contract with you |
| Let buyers and sellers reach each other | Contact channels, messages | Our contract with you |
| Keep the marketplace safe: screen ads and photos, detect fraud and ban evasion, handle reports | Ads, messages, enforcement records, technical records | Our legitimate interest in a safe marketplace; legal duty where one applies |
| Open the site on ads near you | Approximate location, the area you chose | Our legitimate interest |
| Move the map pin to where you are, when you ask | Your device's position, read once | Your consent, given in your browser's prompt |
| Understand which categories and areas are used | Usage counts | Our legitimate interest |
| Keep proof of what you agreed to and certified | Acceptance and certification records | Our legitimate interest in defending legal claims |
| Send service messages: sign-in emails and notices of changes to the Terms | Email address | Our contract with you |

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. We send no marketing email.

## 5. Decisions made by automated systems

- **Screening.** Ads and photos are screened by automated systems, including AI. These systems decide whether an ad is shown, shown with reduced reach, held or rejected, and they can freeze an account after a severe breach.
- **Your safeguards.** Every rejection gives its reason. You can appeal once, and you can ask for a person to review an enforcement action.
- **Writing help.** The tool that drafts a title or description also uses AI. It receives only what you entered in that ad.

## 6. Who we share it with

- **The public.** Your ads and your seller name are public. Section 7 gives the detail.
- **Signed-in users.** The contact channels you choose to show.
- **Authorities and legal claims.** When the law requires it, or on a valid order. Also where it is needed to protect our rights or the safety of users or the public, or to investigate fraud or abuse.
- **A new owner.** If ethio.com is merged with or sold to another company, your data may pass to that company. We tell you before that happens and before a different privacy policy applies to it.
- **Companies that process data for us**, under contract and only for the purpose shown:

| Company | What it does for us |
| --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database, sign-in and file storage |
| Cloudflare | Delivers and protects the site; tells us the approximate location of a visit |
| Google | Sign-in with Google, if you choose it; its Gemini AI screens ads and photos and drafts text |
| Telegram | Sign-in with Telegram, if you choose it |
| OpenStreetMap | Map pictures and address lookup when you place a map pin; your device loads map pictures directly from its servers |
| Resend | Sends our emails |
| Lovable | Runs the application |

Public ads also appear on our own channels and pages on other services, such as Telegram and Facebook, and those services then hold a copy of the ad. We may change these companies, and this list always shows the current ones. Nobody else receives your personal data.

Ads can link to other sites, for example to a video. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

## 7. What is public

- **Public.** Your ads, including photos, price, the places where they are shown and any map pin, and your seller name. Ads are copied to servers around the world so that they load quickly, and search engines may index them. We also post public ads on our own channels and pages on other services, such as Telegram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok.
- **Signed-in users only.** The contact channels you choose to show.
- **Never shown.** Your first and last name and your email address.
- **Behind the name.** We always know which account is behind a seller name.

## 8. Where data is stored

Personal data is stored on servers in the United States. If you live in another country, using ethio.com means your data is transferred there. We send it only to companies that are bound by contract to protect it.

We may later store the data of users in some countries, Ethiopia first, on servers inside those countries. This policy will be updated before that happens.

## 9. How long we keep it

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| An ad that is running | While it runs |
| An ad marked sold or no longer available | Public for 30 days with a label, then in your own list for 12 months |
| An expired ad | In your own list for 12 months |
| An ad you delete | 12 months out of public view, then erased |
| An ad or account under a report, an enforcement action or an official request | Until the matter closes, plus 3 years |
| A closed account | Its ads as above; the account's identity for 12 months, then erased |
| A banned account | A minimal record of the ban and its reason, so that the ban can be enforced |
| Records of what you accepted and certified | Kept with the account or the ad, and erased with it |
| In-app messages | As long as the ad they are about. After that ad is deleted, 12 months. Under a report, a dispute or an official request, until the matter closes plus 3 years |
| Technical records | 12 months, then erased. Server logs held by the hosting companies are kept for the periods those companies set |
| The approximate-location guess | Not stored |

Copies kept out of public view can be opened only by staff with a specific permission, and every such access is logged.

## 10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your personal data, including the record of what you accepted and certified
- correct it
- delete it
- give it to you in a form you can take elsewhere
- restrict how we use it
- stop a use you object to, including a decision made by an automated system, which a person will then review
- withdraw a consent you gave, for example for reading your device's position

Write to privacy@ethio.com. Before we act, we confirm that the request comes from you: by a fresh sign-in if you can sign in, or by other proof if you cannot. We answer within 30 days, or sooner where the law of your country sets a shorter time. Where a request is complex, we may take up to 60 days more, and we tell you so within the first 30 days. Deletion has limits: section 9 lists what we keep for a time and why. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

**To delete your data.** You can delete any ad from your own list. To delete your account and everything in it, write to privacy@ethio.com from the email address of the account.

**United States residents.** We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising on other sites, so there is nothing to opt out of. The rights above apply to you as well.

## 11. Children

ethio.com is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and erase its data, apart from the minimal record section 9 describes.

## 12. Security and breaches

- **Protection.** Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and backups are encrypted. Access by our staff is controlled and logged. Everything users submit is screened before it is stored. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that a breach will never happen.
- **If something goes wrong.** We tell the relevant authority and the people affected within 72 hours of learning of a breach of personal data.
- **Reporting a weakness.** Write to security@ethio.com.

## 13. Cookies and storage on your device

- **Two preference cookies.** `ethio_lang_star` holds the default language you chose. `ethio_area` holds the browsing area you chose. Neither identifies you, and neither is set from your network address. Choosing again or clearing cookies removes them.
- **Sign-in.** While you are signed in, your browser stores a session token so that you stay signed in.
- **Security check.** The check that keeps robots out of sign-up may set a cookie of its own.
- **Nothing else.** We use no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking. Because we do not track you across other sites, the site works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

## 14. Changes to this policy

Each version has a number and a date. When we make a material change, we tell you by email where we hold your address, at least 30 days before it takes effect, and you are asked to accept the new version the next time you sign in.
