## How to read these drafts

The doc has six tabs. This one holds the publishing statement and the open points. Two hold the English text of the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, and two hold their Amharic translations. The last one reviews the 2020 documents section by section.

- **Source.** Each clause comes from the project's source ledger (`docs/spec/tos-privacy-source.md`, clauses A1 to A14 and B1 to B12) or from a decision you made on 4 October: 18 or older with no date of birth, versioned acceptance, the publishing certification, and the retention periods. Clauses carried over from the 2020 documents are listed in the last tab.
- **Brackets.** Text in \[SQUARE BRACKETS\] is a fact I do not have or a decision that is still yours. Each one is listed in the table below.
- **Status.** These are pre-counsel drafts. Your standing ruling is that a lawyer reviews them at the Ethiopia-entity milestone, and that this does not block global launch.
- **Language.** The English text is the binding one. The two Amharic tabs and the Amharic statement below translate the text you agreed on 4 October. A native speaker should read them before launch, as the launch checklist already requires for all Amharic copy.
- **Where they go.** When the Legal section of the admin console is built, each becomes version 1 of its document there.

## Publishing statement

This is the global statement a seller certifies with one tick before publishing, editing or renewing an ad. Country statements, where you add them, appear under it.

> I certify that:
>
> 1. Everything in this ad is true and complete, and the photos show what I am offering.
> 2. I have the right to offer this item or service and to use the photos and text in this ad, and offering it breaks no law in the places where the ad is shown.
> 3. The ad follows the Terms of Service and the posting rules of ethio.com.
> 4. I alone, or the business I act for, am responsible for this ad and for any deal that follows from it.
> 5. ethio.com only displays the ad. It is not a party to any deal and is not liable for it, as the Terms of Service set out.

The tick reads: "I certify the above."

Six further points are for the lawyer, not for you:

- Sending data from the EU or the UK to the United States needs a recognised legal mechanism, and the draft does not name one.
- Which countries the sanctions line in Terms 2 closes to users.
- Whether the liability limit and the exclusion of warranties in Terms 12 must be printed more prominently to hold up in a United States court.
- Whether the California privacy law applies. It has size thresholds that ethio.com is unlikely to meet at launch.
- Whether the copyright agent's phone number must also be printed on the site. The law lists it, and Craigslist, eBay and OfferUp leave it off their terms pages.
- How far the right in Terms 5 to act without notice holds for users in the EU and the UK, where a platform must give reasons.
